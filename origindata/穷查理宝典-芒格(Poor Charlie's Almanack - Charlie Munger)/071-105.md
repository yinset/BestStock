Berkshire's Insurance Operations

Reinsurance is not as much of a commodity business as it might appear. We have a reputational advantage, though it's not as big as it should be.

I do think we get some advantage in reinsurance because people trust our willingness and ability to pay, so it's not a commodity. I think we have some special talents. That being said, I think it's dangerous to rely on special methods, better to own lots of monopolistic businesses with unregulated prices. But that's not the world today. We have made money exercising our talents and will continue to do so.

I'm glad we have insurance, though it's not a no-brainer, I'm warning you. We have to be smart to make this work.
